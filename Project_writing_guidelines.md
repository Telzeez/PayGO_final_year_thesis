## PROJECT/TECHNICAL REPORT WRITING

Final year project work is an important aspect of the programme, and the departmental format of project reports presentation is as follows:

- 1. The contents of the report should be arranged in the following order:

i ii

Certification

iii Dedication (If any)

iv Acknowledgement

v Abstract (not more than a page)

vi Table of contents

vii List of Abbreviations

viii List of Figures

ix List of Tables

x Introduction

xi The main body of the report (which could be several chapters)

xii Conclusion and Recommendation.

xiii Appendices

xiv References

- 2. The title page should contain the title of the project and the name of the author as well as the following statement:

“A PROJECT REPORT SUBMITTED TO THE DEPARTMENT OF ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONIC ENGINEERING, FACULTY OF TECHNOLOGY, UNIVERSITY OF IBADAN, IN PARTIAL FULFILMENT OF THE REQUIREMENT FOR THE AWARD OF BACHELOR OF SCIENCE DEGREE IN ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONIC ENGINEERING OF THE UNIVERSITY OF IBADAN.”

The bottom of the page should contain the month and year of submission.

- 3. The certification page should start with “CERTIFICATION”

Then underneath should be “This is to certify that the project upon which this report is based was carried out by (name of student in capital letters) in the department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Faculty of Technology, University of Ibadan.”

Project Supervisor: - …………………………………………...

Signature with dates: - ………………………………………….

Head of Department: - …………………………………………

Signature with dates: - ………………………………………….

- 1. The page numbers from Title Page up to list of Tables should be in Roman Figures (although actual page printing should start from the certification page) The page numbers in Arabic numerals should start from the “Introduction”

- 2. The typing should be double-spacing on A4 paper. The top margin should be 4cm, the bottom margin 2cm, left hand 4cm and the right-hand margin 1.5cm.

Title Page


- 6. Figures and diagrams should be carefully drawn. Free hand drawings are not acceptable. Proper lettering should be done. Graph sheets are not allowed. All figures appear immediately (or at the next convenient space) after being cited. The captions are to be at the bottom of diagrams.

- 7. All tables must be given title and should be at top of the table.

- 8. Equations should be as much as possible typed using in-built or plugin equation editor and where it becomes impossible, equations could be carefully hand – written.

- 9. References

All References must be cited in the text and numbered consecutively as they appear in the text. Text cited should be checked carefully against the Reference list to avoid omissions or discrepancies in spellings or dates.

In the text: (a) Each entry in the Reference List must have a citation in the text.

- (b) Citations are enclosed in square brackets in the form of the number to be cited e.g. Possible applications of these algorithms include speech [2] – [6], image [7] and line drawing encoding [8], and contour extraction [9].

In the Reference List:

- (a) The References should be listed according to the number given to them in the text.

(b) The following information should be given in each reference.

- 1. Names of all the authors in Capital letters

- 2. Title of the paper.

- 3. Title of the Journal.

- 4. Year of Publication and Volume Number.

- 5. First and last page numbers

For example; ANDERSON, J. B, and MOHAN, S.: “Sequential Coding algorithms:

A survey and cost analysis”, IEEE Trans. Comm.., FEB. 1984, Vol. Com –32, pp 169-176.

- 10. The front cover should contain the title of the project and the name of the author as well as the “Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering

Faculty of Technology

University of Ibadan”.

The bottom of the page should contain the month and year of submission.

- 11. At the spine the following should be written:

“B. Sc. Electrical /Electronic Eng. (Year of submission) (Name of author.)”

- 12. Finally, the cover colour is the Departmental colour which is at present Golden Yellow.
